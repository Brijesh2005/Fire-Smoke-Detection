ResNet-50 Deep Residual Pipeline
Transfer Learning Backbone

The model utilizes a deep residual convolutional network (ResNet-50) initialized with ImageNet weights to extract multi-scale spatial and color gradients representative of combustion and dispersion.

[TENSOR PIPELINE]
Input Image: [Batch, 3, 224, 224] (RGB)
↓ Normalized (ImageNet Mean/Std)
ResNet-50 Backbone → 2,048-dim Feature Map
↓ Linear(2048 → 128) + ReLU()
↓ Linear(128 → 3)
↓ Softmax(dim=1) → Probability Spectrum [Fire, Neutral, Smoke]
Classification Classes:
  • Fire: Open active flames, thermal radiation, combustion cores.
  • Smoke: Aerosol dispersion, rising smoke plumes, early smoldering.
  • Neutral: Standard ambient indoor/outdoor environments devoid of combustion.
